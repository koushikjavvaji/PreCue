# AURORA HEALTH FOODS - Q2 FY2026 BUSINESS REVIEW

(Fictional sample document for testing PreCue. All companies and numbers are made up.)

## 1. Executive Summary

Aurora Health Foods delivered its strongest quarter so far. Revenue for Q2 FY2026 was ₹148.6 crore, up 13.3% from ₹131.2 crore in Q1 FY2026 and up 32.2% from ₹112.4 crore in Q2 FY2025. EBITDA was ₹19.3 crore, and net profit rose to ₹9.6 crore from ₹6.8 crore in Q1.

Growth was led by e-commerce and the South region. Our millet range now brings in 41% of revenue. We reached 1.85 lakh retail outlets and launched 18 new products during the quarter.

## 2. Financial Performance

Gross margin improved to 38.5% in Q2 from 36.9% in Q1, as we bought grain earlier in the season and reduced wastage. EBITDA margin rose to 13.0% from 11.2% in Q1. Net profit margin was 6.5%.

Operating expenses (excluding raw materials) were ₹38.0 crore. Finance costs were ₹1.4 crore. Cash and bank balances at the end of the quarter stood at ₹54.3 crore, and the company has no long-term debt.

|---|---|---|---|

| Gross margin | 35.1% | 36.9% | 38.5% |
| EBITDA | ₹10.9 crore | ₹14.7 crore | ₹19.3 crore |
| EBITDA margin | 9.7% | 11.2% | 13.0% |
| Net profit | ₹4.1 crore | ₹6.8 crore | ₹9.6 crore |

## 3. Revenue by Channel

Modern trade (supermarket chains) remained our largest channel at ₹52.0 crore, or 35% of revenue. General trade (neighbourhood stores) contributed ₹44.6 crore, which is 30% of revenue. E-commerce, including quick-commerce apps, contributed ₹37.2 crore (25%) and grew 41% year on year. Exports contributed ₹14.8 crore (10%) and grew 18% year on year, mainly to the UAE and the UK.

## 4. Regional Performance

The South region was our largest market with revenue of ₹50.5 crore, growing 38% year on year. North contributed ₹46.1 crore (up 29%), West contributed ₹34.2 crore (up 31%) and East contributed ₹17.8 crore (up 24%). East is still our smallest region, and we plan to add 220 distributors there in the second half of the year.

## 5. Products

The millet range earned ₹61.0 crore in Q2, which is 41% of total revenue. Snacks earned ₹55.0 crore and beverages earned ₹32.6 crore. We now sell 214 products (SKUs), including 18 new products launched in Q2. Our best-selling product, Ragi Crunch, sold 2.4 million packs in the quarter. Beverages grew the slowest, at 9% year on year.

## 6. Customers and Distribution

Our products are now available in 1.85 lakh retail outlets, up from 1.62 lakh in Q1. We work with 640 distributors. The repeat purchase rate on e-commerce was 46%, and the average order value on our own website was ₹780. Customer complaints fell to 0.6 per 10,000 packs sold, from 0.9 in Q1.

## 7. Operations and Supply Chain

We run 3 manufacturing plants. Capacity utilization rose to 78% in Q2 from 71% in Q1. On-time-in-full (OTIF) deliveries to customers reached 94.2%. Inventory days fell to 41 from 47 in Q1, and wastage fell to 1.8% of production from 2.6% in Q1. We spent ₹2.1 crore this quarter on a one-time upgrade of the Mysuru plant's packaging line.

## 8. People

Headcount grew to 1,240 employees at the end of Q2, from 1,175 at the end of Q1. We hired 96 people in the quarter. Annualized attrition was 14.5%, down from 17.0% in FY2025. Women make up 37% of our workforce and 29% of our managers.

## 9. Marketing

Marketing spend was ₹11.9 crore, or 8.0% of revenue. Brand awareness in the top 8 cities rose to 54% from 47% in Q1, after our cricket-season campaign. Our social media channels have 1.3 million followers.

## 10. Sustainability

Renewable sources now provide 42% of the energy used in our plants. We recycled 1,150 tonnes of plastic packaging in Q2 under our extended producer responsibility plan, and water use per tonne of product fell 9% compared with Q1.

## 11. Risks

Millet prices rose 12% during Q2, and a further rise would hurt gross margin. A possible change in GST rates on packaged snacks could raise prices for customers. Competition in quick-commerce is growing, and two large brands have cut prices by about 10%.

## 12. Outlook and Targets

For Q3 FY2026 we target revenue of ₹165 crore and an EBITDA margin of 13.5%. For the full year FY2026, our revenue guidance is ₹600 crore and our EBITDA margin target is 14%. We plan to reach 2.2 lakh retail outlets by March 2026. A new plant in Odisha, with a capital cost of ₹85 crore, will start production in Q1 FY2027.
