How it works

Present as usual. PreCue has your back.

Before the meeting, PreCue reads your report once and lists every number in it. During the meeting, it does three things, again and again.

Listens

Turns your words into text as you speak, including numbers said as words ("ninety-six", "two point four million").

live

Checks

Finds the right part of your report and compares the number: same metric, same period, same direction, any unit.

about 2 seconds

Whispers

If you got it wrong, a private pop-up shows the right number and a voice says it in your earphones. Once per mistake.

only when wrong
Built for real meetings

Smart about numbers. Silent when you are right.

A wrong alert in a meeting is worse than none, so PreCue only speaks up when it is sure.

Understands units

Lakh, crore, million, percent. The same amount said another way is not a mistake.

"24 lakh packs" = 2.4 million in the report ✓

Knows the period

This quarter, last quarter, a target. Last quarter's number said as this quarter's is caught.

"Margin this quarter was 36.9%" → it was 38.5%

Checks the direction

The right number with the wrong story is still wrong.

"Attrition rose to 14.5%" → it went down, not up

Suggests what is next

Pause for a few seconds and PreCue points to a section of your report you have not covered yet.

Safe with any file

Hidden instructions inside a document cannot change its alerts, and PreCue warns you when a file has them.

Fast

Ready in seconds for a report it has seen before. Searching a 700-part report takes about 70 milliseconds.

Privacy

No PreCue server. Nothing comes to us.

PreCue runs on your computer. It uses three AI services with keys that you create and own, so you can see exactly where your data goes.

Your voice → DeepgramTo turn speech into text, and to speak the whisper.
Your report → Gemini, onceTo list the numbers in it before the meeting. Cached on your computer after that.
One sentence and the matching part → GroqTo check a claim. Only when you say a number.

What stays on your computer

Your keys (in a file only your user account can read), your reports, the search index and the transcript. The pop-up is hidden from screen sharing, so the people in your meeting see your slides, not your corrections.

Use your own judgement for confidential documents: they are processed by the AI services above under their terms.

FAQ

Questions

Can the people in my meeting see or hear PreCue?

No. On macOS and Windows the pop-up is left out of screen sharing and screenshots, and whispers go to your earphones. On laptop speakers others might hear the whisper, so PreCue warns you to use earphones.

Does it work with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. PreCue does not connect to the meeting app at all: it listens to your microphone and shows its pop-up on top of whatever is on your screen, including full-screen slides.

Is it really free?

Yes. PreCue is open source, and the three AI services have free tiers that are enough for several meetings a day. There is no PreCue account or subscription.

What if I say a number that is not in my report?

PreCue stays silent. It only corrects you when your report clearly gives a different value for the same thing and the same period.

Which languages does it understand?

English, including Indian number words like lakh and crore. Your report can be a PDF, Word, PowerPoint, text or Markdown file.

What happens if my internet drops?

PreCue reconnects by itself, and the meeting goes on. A check that would arrive too late is skipped rather than shown out of context.
